How rankings and availability work

Top performers rank by recent per-appearance production: PTS + REB + AST + STL + BLK − TOV. Surging ranks the largest positive change in that same index. This is a transparent box-score proxy, not your fantasy league’s scoring system or a complete basketball rating.

Four-game rankings require at least 3 recorded appearances and 10 minutes per appearance in a full four-team-game window. Surging also requires at least 3 previous appearances. Weekly comparisons use two complete Monday–Sunday weeks and at least 2 appearances in each window for surging. Team changes exclude players from surge rankings.

Production is per recorded appearance; counting-stat totals are also shown for fantasy volume. Shooting percentages use total makes and attempts, and their changes are percentage points. Defense covers box-score events, not overall defensive impact.

“No recorded appearance” does not establish a DNP-CD, injury absence, or roster membership. DNP-CD reasons are not collected by the current source. Injury reports show reported statuses and coverage, not confirmed reasons for missing a game.
